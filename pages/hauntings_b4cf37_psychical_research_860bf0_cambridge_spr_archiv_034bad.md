## Introduction

The Society for [Psychical Research]({{ 'research-files/' | relative_url }}) (SPR) archive held at Cambridge University Library is one of the most important surviving evidence collections for the study of hauntings. Rather than preserving ghost stories as folklore alone, it preserves the paper trail behind them: witness letters, investigator correspondence, newspaper cuttings, photographs, site notes, committee reports, catalogues and unpublished case files. The result is an unusually detailed record of how alleged hauntings were reported, investigated, challenged and remembered. For researchers interested in apparitions, haunted houses and [poltergeist claims]({{ 'moving-objects/' | relative_url }}), the archive functions less as a repository of conclusions than as a repository of evidence trails that can be revisited decades later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

## What the SPR Archive Preserves

The archive contains material accumulated since the founding of the SPR in 1882. Cambridge University Library describes the society as having established systematic investigations into [apparitions]({{ 'apparitions/' | relative_url }}), haunted houses and related phenomena, while the archive itself includes correspondence, manuscripts, photographs, journals and case records generated during those investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/collections/departments/archives-modern-and-medieval-manuscripts-and-university-archives-0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.cam.ac.uk">[Cambridge University Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.cam.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The six initial areas of study were Thought-Transference...Read more...</span></span></span>

For haunting-related research, the archive preserves several layers of evidence that are often lost elsewhere:

* Internal correspondence discussing credibility and possible explanations.
* Newspaper coverage collected as the case unfolded.

This layered structure is important because haunting claims often evolve through [retelling]({{ 'retelling/' | relative_url }}). A newspaper article may simplify events, while a later book may emphasise particular incidents. The archive preserves intermediate stages, allowing researchers to compare what witnesses originally said with what later became part of public legend.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

The scale of the collection also matters. The SPR archive extends across roughly 160 linear metres of manuscripts and related material, covering apparitions, [hauntings]({{ 'hauntings/' | relative_url }}), poltergeists and other psychical investigations. Such breadth makes it possible to compare cases across different periods rather than treating each haunting as an isolated story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

## Why Unpublished Cases Matter

Published haunting accounts naturally attract attention, but unpublished files are often more valuable as evidence.

When a case reaches publication, it has already passed through stages of selection, editing and interpretation. By contrast, archive files frequently preserve uncertainty. Witnesses contradict one another. Investigators disagree. Alternative explanations appear alongside paranormal interpretations. Some cases stop abruptly because evidence dries up rather than because a conclusion is reached.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

The Cambridge archive includes nominal indexes and case-box systems that preserve reports which never became famous. These records demonstrate how many haunting claims remained local or unresolved. Instead of presenting only spectacular cases, they reveal the ordinary investigative workload behind psychical research: letters from householders, requests for advice, preliminary inquiries and abandoned investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archivesearch.lib.cam.ac.uk/repositories/2/archival_objects/635597" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archivesearch.lib.cam.ac.uk">[ArchiveSearch]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archivesearch.lib.cam.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">archival objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Box Cases Nominal Index, c.1882-1900 - ArchiveSearchCambridge University Library · Society for Psychical Research archive · SPR...</span></span></span>

For historians, these unpublished files help answer questions that popular ghost narratives cannot. They show what information investigators actually gathered, which claims they considered weak, and how evidential standards changed over time. The archive therefore records not only hauntings but also the development of methods used to examine them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/collections/departments/archives-modern-and-medieval-manuscripts-and-university-archives-0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lib.cam.ac.uk">[Cambridge University Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lib.cam.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The six initial areas of study were Thought-Transference...Read more...</span></span></span>

## How a Haunting Becomes an Archive File

The archive reveals a recurring pattern in SPR investigations.

A report would typically begin with a letter, witness statement or referral. Investigators then gathered supporting testimony, correspondence and press coverage. Notes were exchanged between committee members. Some cases generated site visits, sketches, photographs or recordings. Material accumulated over months or years before being stored in case files and eventually transferred into archival collections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

What survives is therefore not a single piece of evidence but a chain of evidence. Researchers can follow a haunting from first report to later reinterpretation, identifying where claims strengthened, weakened or changed. This process is particularly valuable in controversial cases where different investigators reached different conclusions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

The archive's significance lies in this continuity. A haunting may remain disputed, but the documentary trail itself becomes available for inspection long after witnesses have died and locations have changed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

## Borley Rectory and the Archive of Dispute

Few haunting cases illustrate the value of archival preservation better than [Borley]({{ 'borley/' | relative_url }}) Rectory.

Borley became one of the most famous haunted-house claims in Britain, generating investigations, books and decades of controversy. Yet the historical importance of the case now rests largely on the survival of documentary records. Correspondence, reports and critical reassessments allow later researchers to reconstruct how the haunting narrative developed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>

The archive preserves evidence not only from proponents but also from critics. After the death of investigator [Harry Price]({{ 'harry-price/' | relative_url }}), SPR researchers including Eric Dingwall, K. M. Goldney and Trevor Hall re-examined the material and published a critical assessment that challenged earlier conclusions. Their work became part of the continuing documentary record rather than replacing it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borley_Rectory" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Borley Rectory</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Borley Rectory</span></span></span>

As a result, Borley survives in the archive not as a settled mystery but as an ongoing argument. ## How Later Researchers Use the Record Trail

The archive's greatest value may be its ability to support re-examination.

Researchers studying hauntings rarely encounter repeatable laboratory evidence. What they often have instead are historical records. The Cambridge SPR archive allows those records to be checked against one another. A witness statement can be compared with newspaper coverage. A published claim can be compared with an investigator's private correspondence. A celebrated incident can be traced back to its earliest surviving description.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/archive-society-psychical-research/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk">[Psi Encyclopedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Psi Encyclopedia Archive of the Society for Psychical Research</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Psi EncyclopediaArchive of the Society for Psychical Research - Psi EncyclopediaMar 24, 2023 — The documents and audio-visual material ar...</span></span></span>
