## Introduction

[Ghost tours]({{ 'ghost-tours/' | relative_url }}) do more than entertain visitors with tales of [apparitions]({{ 'apparitions/' | relative_url }}) and unexplained events. They help create, preserve and market a place's haunted identity. In many towns and cities, haunted reputation has become a cultural and economic asset: guides retell local legends, visitors share their experiences, media coverage amplifies the stories, and the destination becomes increasingly associated with ghosts. Over time, the reputation itself can become more influential than the original reports.

This process sits at the intersection of memory, folklore and tourism. Haunted places attract visitors seeking excitement, history, mystery or a connection with the past. In turn, tourism generates new stories, photographs, reviews and personal accounts that reinforce the location's status as haunted. Ghost tourism therefore provides a clear example of how [retelling]({{ 'retelling/' | relative_url }}) can transform local legends into enduring public identities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://campus-fryslan.studenttheses.ub.rug.nl/358/1/MA%20S5015413%20AD%20Drebes.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: campus-fryslan.studenttheses.ub.rug.nl">[Studenttheses Campus Fryslan+2Urbanities]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">campus-fryslan.studenttheses.ub.rug.nl</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Studenttheses Campus FryslanMaster Thesis - Studenttheses Campus FryslanJune 29, 2023 — by A Drebes · 2023 — Common activities regarding...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 29, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why Visitors Seek Haunted Places

People rarely join a ghost tour solely because they are convinced ghosts exist. Research on ghost tourism and related forms of dark tourism suggests that visitors are motivated by a mixture of curiosity, entertainment, historical interest, thrill-seeking and a desire to experience places associated with mystery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/353101143_Dark_tourism_motivations_and_visit_intentions_of_tourists" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate+2Helvia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) Dark tourism: motivations and visit intentions of touristsResults of this study indicate that tourists are curious, int...</span></span></span>

A haunted location offers something unusual compared with conventional heritage tourism. Visitors are invited to imagine hidden layers of the past while exploring streets, castles, cemeteries or historic buildings. The attraction often lies in uncertainty. A participant does not need to believe a story completely to enjoy the possibility that it might be true. Researchers have noted that ghost tourism frequently combines education, folklore and playful suspense in ways that differ from other forms of dark tourism focused primarily on tragedy or death.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.revenantjournal.com/contents/introduction-5/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: revenantjournal.com">[Revenant Journal+2Urbanities]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">revenantjournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Revenant Journal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">IntroductionSupernatural tourism, such as ghost walks, ghost tourism offers a different experience to traditional dark tourism. virtual s...</span></span></span>

For many visitors, the appeal is experiential rather than evidential. The [atmosphere]({{ 'atmosphere/' | relative_url }}) of a narrow alley, an old prison or a centuries-old castle can make a story feel immediate. The setting allows people to engage emotionally with local history while also enjoying the excitement of a supernatural narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372854055_An_exploratory_study_of_the_future_paranormal_tourist_experience_on_ghost_tours" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate+2OpenAIR@RGU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) An exploratory study of the future paranormal tourist...The purpose of this study is to explore the paranormal tourist...</span></span></span>

## How Guides Turn Local History into Haunted Reputation

Ghost tours rely on storytelling. Guides typically combine documented historical events with [local folklore]({{ 'local-folklore/' | relative_url }}), witness reports and legends that have accumulated over time. The result is not simply a history lesson and not simply a ghost story. It is a performance that connects the two.

Studies of [ghost walks]({{ 'ghost-walks/' | relative_url }}) have found that guides often use haunting narratives to make urban history memorable and accessible. Rather than presenting a list of dates and facts, they link historical locations to dramatic stories involving murder, disease, accidents, unexplained sightings or local characters whose legends have survived long after their deaths.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.anthrojournal-urbanities.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/5-Weston.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: anthrojournal-urbanities.com">[Urbanities+2Emerald Publishing]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">anthrojournal-urbanities.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5 Weston</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UrbanitiesDeath and Living Memories in the Dark Tourism of British...by GWJWH Cornish · 2019 · Cited by 11 — This article explores the r...</span></span></span>

This storytelling has several effects:

* It gives visitors a narrative framework for interpreting a place.
* It preserves local legends that might otherwise disappear.
* It creates emotional engagement with historical sites.
* It encourages visitors to remember and retell what they have heard.

Because the stories are repeated nightly, season after season, they become part of the destination's public identity. Even sceptical visitors often leave remembering the haunting narrative more vividly than the historical details that inspired it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.anthrojournal-urbanities.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/5-Weston.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: anthrojournal-urbanities.com">[Urbanities+2Revenant Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">anthrojournal-urbanities.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5 Weston</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UrbanitiesDeath and Living Memories in the Dark Tourism of British...by GWJWH Cornish · 2019 · Cited by 11 — This article explores the r...</span></span></span>

Commercial operators frequently recognise this value. Ghost tours, paranormal events and haunted heritage experiences are marketed as distinctive attractions that differentiate one destination from another. In some places, haunted branding has become a significant tourism strategy rather than a minor curiosity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.emerald.com/qmr/article/21/2/232/360890/Walking-on-the-light-sideInvestigating-the-world" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: emerald.com">[Emerald Publishing+2Studenttheses Campus Fryslan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">emerald.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Emerald PublishingWalking on the light side: Investigating the world of ghost tour...The purpose of this paper is to examine the phenome...</span></span></span>

## Reputation Loops: How Tourism Creates More Haunted Stories

The most important mechanism in ghost tourism is the reputation loop.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://dataintelo.com/report/ghost-tourism-market" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dataintelo.com">[dataintelo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dataintelo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ghost tourism market</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Research Report 2034The ghost tourism market was valued at $3.8 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $8.1 billion by 2034, growing a...</span></span></span>

A location gains a haunted reputation through stories. That reputation attracts visitors. Visitors arrive expecting unusual experiences and paying close attention to atmospheric details. Some then report strange feelings, unexplained sounds or coincidences. Those reports become new material for guides, websites, social media posts and future visitors. The cycle repeats.

Researchers examining ghost tourism have noted that supernatural legends increasingly become commercialised through organised tours and related experiences. What begins as folklore can develop into an ongoing system of storytelling sustained by visitor participation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rgu-repository.worktribe.com/output/451549/the-allure-of-dark-tourism-legend-tripping-and-ghost-seeking-in-dark-places" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rgu-repository.worktribe.com">[OpenAIR@RGU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rgu-repository.worktribe.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the allure of dark tourism legend tripping and ghost seeking in dark places</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">OpenAIR@RGUThe allure of dark tourism: legend tripping and ghost seeking...by R Ironside · 2018 · Cited by 50 — Legend tripping is a com...</span></span></span>

Modern technology accelerates this process. Visitors upload photographs, videos and personal accounts to social media platforms. Online reviews frequently mention eerie sensations, unexpected events or favourite ghost stories. Even when the experiences are ambiguous, they contribute to the site's haunted image.

As a result, haunted reputation is not merely inherited from the past. It is continuously produced in the present. Each generation of visitors helps renew the legend through retelling and reinterpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372854055_An_exploratory_study_of_the_future_paranormal_tourist_experience_on_ghost_tours" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate+2OpenAIR@RGU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) An exploratory study of the future paranormal tourist...The purpose of this study is to explore the paranormal tourist...</span></span></span>

## Cities That Trade on Their Ghostly Identity

Some destinations have integrated ghost tourism deeply into their cultural and economic life.

Edinburgh provides a well-known example. The city's history of plague outbreaks, executions, underground [vaults]({{ 'vaults/' | relative_url }}) and folklore has supported a large ghost-tour industry. ## How Ghost Tours Keep Haunted Stories Alive

Ghost tours are among the most effective mechanisms for preserving and spreading haunted reputation. They transform local memories, rumours and folklore into experiences that can be consumed, repeated and shared. Every guided walk creates a temporary community of listeners who carry those stories away and potentially retell them elsewhere.

In this sense, haunted reputation is not simply a record of past claims. It is an active social process. Guides transmit stories, visitors reinterpret them, businesses market them and destinations adopt them as part of their identity. The haunting survives not because it remains unchanged, but because each retelling gives it a new audience.

Ghost tourism therefore demonstrates how memory, storytelling and commerce work together. A haunted place attracts visitors because of its stories, and those visitors help ensure that the stories continue to live.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.anthrojournal-urbanities.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/5-Weston.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: anthrojournal-urbanities.com">[Studenttheses Campus Fryslan+3Urbanities+3OpenAIR@RGU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">anthrojournal-urbanities.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5 Weston</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UrbanitiesDeath and Living Memories in the Dark Tourism of British...by GWJWH Cornish · 2019 · Cited by 11 — This article explores the r...</span></span></span>
