## Introduction

[Harry Price]({{ 'harry-price/' | relative_url }})’s 1922 [investigation]({{ 'investigation/' | relative_url }}) of the spirit photographer William Hope is one of the clearest examples of the anti-fraud methods that made Price influential in paranormal research. At a time when many grieving families believed photographs could capture the images of deceased relatives, Hope’s pictures appeared to provide visual proof of survival after death. Price approached the claim differently. Drawing on his knowledge of photography, conjuring and deception, he designed a test intended not to argue about beliefs but to determine whether Hope was using the photographic materials supplied to him. The resulting exposure became a landmark case because it focused on physical evidence and controlled testing rather than witness testimony alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/photographic-plates-and-spirit-fakes-remembering-harry-prices-investigation-of-william-hopes-spirit-photography-at-its-centenary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk">[Science Museum Group Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Museum Group Journalremembering Harry Price&#x27;s investigation of William Hope&#x27;s...26 Nov 2022 — Photographic plates and spirit fak...</span></span></span>

## Why Spirit Photographs Persuaded Believers

[Spirit photography]({{ 'fake-ghosts/' | relative_url }}) had a powerful emotional appeal. In the decades after the First World War, many families sought contact with lost relatives, and photographs seemed more convincing than spoken messages from mediums. Hope, a member of the Crewe Circle of spiritualists, became one of the best-known practitioners of the technique. His photographs appeared to show ghostly faces or figures hovering near the living sitter, creating what looked like direct visual evidence of communication with the dead.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk/william-hope-spirit-photography/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk">[National Science and Media Museum blog]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">william hope spirit photography</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Science and Media Museum blogThe significance of spiritualism in the work of William Hope30 Jun 2021 — William Hope&#x27;s &#x27;spirit ph...</span></span></span>

Part of Hope’s success came from the apparent transparency of the process. Sitters often observed the handling and development of plates and believed there was little opportunity for manipulation. Because photography was widely associated with mechanical objectivity, many people assumed the camera itself was a neutral witness. This gave spirit photographs a credibility that ordinary séance testimony often lacked.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk/william-hope-spirit-photography/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk">[National Science and Media Museum blog]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">blog.scienceandmediamuseum.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">william hope spirit photography</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Science and Media Museum blogThe significance of spiritualism in the work of William Hope30 Jun 2021 — William Hope&#x27;s &#x27;spirit ph...</span></span></span>

For investigators such as Price, however, the crucial question was not whether the images were emotionally persuasive but whether the photographic procedure could withstand controlled scrutiny.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/photographic-plates-and-spirit-fakes-remembering-harry-prices-investigation-of-william-hopes-spirit-photography-at-its-centenary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk">[Science Museum Group Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Museum Group Journalremembering Harry Price&#x27;s investigation of William Hope&#x27;s...26 Nov 2022 — Photographic plates and spirit fak...</span></span></span>

## How Marked Plates Tested Substitution

Price’s test took place at the British College of Psychic Science in February 1922. Rather than attempting to catch Hope through direct observation alone, Price devised a method aimed at detecting plate substitution. The strategy reflected an important principle in fraud detection: instead of trying to watch every movement, create a condition in which cheating leaves a trace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/photographic-plates-and-spirit-fakes-remembering-harry-prices-investigation-of-william-hopes-spirit-photography-at-its-centenary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk">[Science Museum Group Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Museum Group Journalremembering Harry Price&#x27;s investigation of William Hope&#x27;s...26 Nov 2022 — Photographic plates and spirit fak...</span></span></span>

The test involved secretly marking photographic plates before they were handed to Hope. Price also arranged for specially prepared plates carrying hidden identifying features, including markings associated with the Imperial Dry Plate Company. If Hope genuinely used the supplied materials, those marks should have appeared on the finished photographic products.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://williamhopehodgson.wordpress.com/2012/11/19/william-hope-paranormal-investigator/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: williamhopehodgson.wordpress.com">[william hope hodgson+2Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">williamhopehodgson.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unbeknownst to Hope, Price had secretly marked Hope&#x27;s</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">william hope hodgsonWilliam Hope: Paranormal Investigator!November 19, 2012 — 19 Nov 2012 — The test took place at the British College of...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 19, 2012</span></span></span>

When the photographs were developed, Hope produced the expected spirit images. The problem for him was that the resulting plates lacked the concealed identifiers. The markings Price had introduced were absent, suggesting that the plates returned after the sitting were not the same ones originally supplied. Price concluded that Hope had substituted prepared plates carrying pre-existing spirit images, a technique consistent with photographic fakery through double exposure or related methods.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/photographic-plates-and-spirit-fakes-remembering-harry-prices-investigation-of-william-hopes-spirit-photography-at-its-centenary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk">[Science Museum Group Journal+2Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Museum Group Journalremembering Harry Price&#x27;s investigation of William Hope&#x27;s...26 Nov 2022 — Photographic plates and spirit fak...</span></span></span>

The significance of the test lay in its simplicity. Price did not need to prove exactly how every image had been manufactured. The disappearance of the identifying marks provided evidence that the chain of custody for the photographic materials had been broken. In effect, the experiment transformed a debate about ghosts into a question about whether the physical evidence had been tampered with.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/photographic-plates-and-spirit-fakes-remembering-harry-prices-investigation-of-william-hopes-spirit-photography-at-its-centenary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk">[Science Museum Group Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Museum Group Journalremembering Harry Price&#x27;s investigation of William Hope&#x27;s...26 Nov 2022 — Photographic plates and spirit fak...</span></span></span>

## Why the Exposure Remained Controversial

Although the investigation became famous, it did not end belief in Hope. Prominent spiritualists, including Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, challenged Price’s conclusions and argued that the test was flawed or unfair. Doyle published a defence of spirit photography and maintained that Hope had been wrongly accused.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://photographymuseum.com/sssp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: photographymuseum.com">[Photo Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">photographymuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Photo Museum“Science vs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Seance” Spirit PhotographsMay 3, 2023 — spirit photographs produced by William Hope of Crewe. The dispute illustrates a recurring pattern in paranormal controversies. Evidence that convinces sceptics does not necessarily convince committed believers. For supporters of Hope, the large body of previous spirit photographs carried more weight than a single controlled test. For critics, the controlled test was precisely what mattered because it examined the conditions under which the evidence was produced.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/photographic-plates-and-spirit-fakes-remembering-harry-prices-investigation-of-william-hopes-spirit-photography-at-its-centenary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk">[Science Museum Group Journal]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Museum Group Journalremembering Harry Price&#x27;s investigation of William Hope&#x27;s...26 Nov 2022 — Photographic plates and spirit fak...</span></span></span>

Modern discussions of the case frequently note that Hope continued working after the exposure and retained supporters. The episode therefore reveals both the strengths and limitations of fraud detection: uncovering evidence of deception does not automatically eliminate belief in the underlying claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://photographymuseum.com/sssp/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: photographymuseum.com">[Photo Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">photographymuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Photo Museum“Science vs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Seance” Spirit PhotographsMay 3, 2023 — spirit photographs produced by William Hope of Crewe. ## What the Exposure Says About Price

The Hope investigation highlights the strongest side of Harry Price’s reputation as a paranormal investigator. Unlike investigators who relied primarily on testimony or personal impressions, Price often tried to design practical tests that could reveal trickery. His background in conjuring helped him think in terms of methods, opportunities and concealed substitutions rather than assuming that unusual results required unusual explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Price" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Harry Price</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Harry Price</span></span></span>

Several features of the Hope case became characteristic of Price’s approach:
