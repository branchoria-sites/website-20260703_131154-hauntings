## Introduction

Reports from people who believe they have lived in a haunted house tend to follow remarkably consistent patterns. Rather than dramatic encounters with fully visible ghosts, most accounts centre on recurring everyday experiences: unexplained sounds, fleeting shadows, voices, objects appearing to move, sensations of touch, unusual temperature changes and electrical disturbances. Surveys of people who consider a home haunted repeatedly find the same small cluster of experiences appearing far more often than spectacular paranormal events. These reports matter because they reveal how haunting narratives are built from ambiguous but memorable experiences that residents struggle to explain. They also show why haunted-house stories persist even among people who are uncertain whether the cause is supernatural. ## Sounds, Shadows and Voices

The most frequently reported feature of an allegedly haunted home is sound. In multiple homeowner surveys, unexplained [footsteps]({{ 'footsteps/' | relative_url }}), knocking, banging, music, voices or movement noises consistently rank above all other experiences. A 2025 Realtor.com survey of people who believed they had lived in a haunted home found that 67% reported strange sounds, while earlier surveys similarly identified unexplained noises as the leading sign of a supposedly haunted property. Footsteps moving through empty rooms, knocks that seem to answer occupants, or voices heard when nobody else is present tend to attract attention because they resemble signs of another person in the house. Reports of disembodied voices are also common. Visual experiences usually come second. Residents frequently describe:

* Shadowy figures seen in peripheral vision.
* Human-shaped forms that disappear when directly observed.
* Brief [apparitions]({{ 'apparitions/' | relative_url }}) in hallways, staircases or doorways.
* A persistent feeling that someone is standing nearby.

Survey findings show that sightings of shadows or apparitions are among the most commonly reported haunted-house experiences, often ranking immediately behind unexplained sounds. ## Objects, Touch and Electrical Effects

Many haunted-home reports involve physical changes in the environment rather than sights or sounds alone. A large proportion of residents who describe a house as haunted say they have witnessed objects moving, disappearing, changing position or falling unexpectedly. ## Why Certain Rooms Feel More Haunted

A striking feature of many haunted-house reports is their localisation. Witnesses often identify particular staircases, bedrooms, hallways, basements or corners of a property as the centre of activity.

## Why Ordinary Details Become Eerie

One reason haunted-home reports are so similar across different places is that they often emerge from ordinary events whose meaning is uncertain. A creaking floor, a flickering bulb, a distant voice from outside or a brief shadow may attract little attention in normal circumstances. In a house already suspected of being haunted, however, the same event can become highly significant.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haunted_house" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Haunted house</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Haunted house</span></span></span>

* **Ambiguous sensory information:** unclear sounds, shadows and fleeting perceptions are easier to interpret in multiple ways.

* **Pattern recognition:** people naturally connect separate incidents into a meaningful narrative.
