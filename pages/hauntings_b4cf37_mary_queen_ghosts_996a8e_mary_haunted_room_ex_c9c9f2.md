## Introduction

Mary, Queen of Scots ghost stories often unfold in a setting that is already heavily scripted before a visitor arrives. A guest books a room said to be haunted by a tragic queen, sees portraits of Mary, hears stories about imprisonment and execution, and learns that previous visitors reported unusual experiences. In that context, expectation itself becomes an important part of the haunting narrative.

This does not mean that every report is false. Rather, research on haunted locations consistently shows that prior beliefs, suggestion and environmental cues can shape how people interpret ordinary sensations. In rooms associated with [Mary legends]({{ 'mary-legends/' | relative_url }}), the question is not only what happened in the room, but what visitors expected might happen before they entered it. Studies of haunted environments and paranormal tourism suggest that expectation can influence attention, memory, emotional reactions and the interpretation of ambiguous events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372854055_An_exploratory_study_of_the_future_paranormal_tourist_experience_on_ghost_tours" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate+2ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Research Gate(PDF) An exploratory study of the future paranormal tourist</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) An exploratory study of the future paranormal tourist...August 2, 2023 — The purpose of this study is to explore the p...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 2, 2023</span></span></span>

## Do Mary legends make rooms feel haunted?

The strongest Mary haunting traditions are attached to specific places rather than random sightings. The Talbot Hotel in Oundle, for example, links its ghost story to architectural elements said to have come from [Fotheringhay]({{ 'fotheringhay/' | relative_url }}) Castle, where Mary was executed. Visitors are often introduced to the story before spending the night, and reports commonly focus on particular stairways, bedrooms or corridors associated with the legend.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dailyrecord.co.uk/news/weird-news/mary-queen-scots-ghost-haunts-36638625" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dailyrecord.co.uk">[Daily Record]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dailyrecord.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">mary queen scots ghost haunts 36638625</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Daily RecordMary, Queen of Scots&#x27; ghost &#x27;haunts hotel built from bricks...3 Feb 2026 — According to legend, her executioner spent the ni...</span></span></span>

Psychologists refer to this as an expectancy effect. When people enter a location believing it is haunted, they become more attentive to unusual sounds, shadows, temperature changes and bodily sensations. Events that might be ignored in an ordinary hotel room can take on paranormal significance in a room already labelled as haunted. Research conducted in allegedly haunted buildings has found that suggestion and pre-existing paranormal beliefs influence both expectations and the reporting of unusual experiences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/282948909_Suggestion_belief_in_the_paranormal_proneness_to_reality_testing_deficits_and_perception_of_an_allegedly_haunted_building" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) Suggestion, belief in the paranormal, proneness to...The present study investigated whether suggestion, level of belie...</span></span></span>

In Mary-themed rooms, the legend provides a ready-made interpretation. A creaking floorboard becomes [footsteps]({{ 'footsteps/' | relative_url }}). A glimpse of movement becomes a woman in period dress. A restless night's sleep becomes evidence of a spiritual presence. The experience feels meaningful because the story already supplies a character and a historical narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.peterheadprison.com/2024/05/06/the-psychology-behind-belief-in-ghosts/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: peterheadprison.com">[Peterhead Prison@WalshMedical]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">peterheadprison.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Peterhead Prison The Psychology Behind Belief in Ghosts</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Peterhead PrisonThe Psychology Behind Belief in GhostsMay 6, 2024 — Ghost stories and paranormal media prime the brain to expect haunting...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 6, 2024</span></span></span>

## How named ghost rooms shape perception

Named ghost rooms are especially powerful because they narrow attention toward a specific expectation.

A visitor entering "Mary's room" or a room advertised as associated with Mary, Queen of Scots, is not simply entering a bedroom. They are entering a place framed by centuries of storytelling. Research on paranormal tourism shows that prior knowledge and anticipation are central components of visitors' experiences at haunted sites. Many participants actively seek an emotionally charged encounter and interpret their surroundings through that lens.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372854055_An_exploratory_study_of_the_future_paranormal_tourist_experience_on_ghost_tours" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Research Gate(PDF) An exploratory study of the future paranormal tourist</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) An exploratory study of the future paranormal tourist...August 2, 2023 — The purpose of this study is to explore the p...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 2, 2023</span></span></span>

* **Suggestibility:** Hearing previous witness accounts can increase the likelihood that later visitors report similar sensations or perceptions.

## Ordinary discomforts that become signs

Many reported haunting experiences begin with sensations that are real but not necessarily paranormal.

Historic buildings associated with Mary legends are often old, acoustically complex and unfamiliar to overnight guests. Such environments naturally produce unexpected sounds, changing temperatures and unusual lighting conditions. Architecture researchers note that long corridors, dim spaces, echoes and ambiguous visual information can make people feel uneasy and encourage misinterpretation of sensory cues.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/how-architecture-affects-fear-and-perception" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalgeographic.com">[National Geographic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalgeographic.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">how architecture affects fear and perception</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National GeographicThe science of why some spaces feel haunted—even...29 Oct 2025 — Experts reveal how architecture—through walls, light...</span></span></span>

In [old buildings]({{ 'old-buildings/' | relative_url }}), mechanical systems, [pipes]({{ 'pipes/' | relative_url }}) and ventilation equipment may contribute to such sensations. ## Why expectation does not automatically explain every report

Expectation effects are powerful, but they are not a complete explanation for every claim.

Many witnesses insist that they experienced something unexpected despite being sceptical or unaware of the story beforehand.
